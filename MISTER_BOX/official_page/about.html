Want to know more about MISTER BOX?

MISTER BOX is more than just a restaurant — it's a hidden culinary experience built around the thrill of surprise. Founded in 2020, every dish is prepared to look mysterious on the outside and deliver an unforgettable flavor adventure on the inside.
Our menu offers a variety of fusion-inspired meals originated in the roots of Mauritius, East Africa, blending traditional recipes with modern twists — each meal is presented in a way that keeps the ingredients a surprise until your first bite.
You don't find us. We find you. MISTER BOX is designed for those who dare to dine differently. Follow our social pages for secret pop-up spots and event nights.
